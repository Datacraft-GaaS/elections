# Campaign Finance Transparency Admin

Django admin console for campaign finance transparency and financial accountability. Manages candidate/party financial disclosures, cash aggregates, evidence packages, and referrals to CBK/FRC (Financial Reporting Centre) for investigation.

## Overview

Module: M22 (Campaign Finance)

Transparency and accountability back-office for public campaign finance oversight. Enables disclosure publication, evidence collection for suspected violations, and coordination with financial regulators (CBK/FRC) for enforcement.

## Key Features

- **Financial Disclosures**: Record candidate and party financial disclosures with source tracking
- **Cash Aggregates**: Aggregate cash flows to detect suspicious patterns
- **Evidence Packaging**: Collect evidence for suspected financial violations
- **CBK/FRC Referral**: Submit evidence packages to Central Bank and Financial Reporting Centre
- **Public Transparency**: Manage disclosure publication timeline and delay schedules
- **Admin Views**: DisclosureAdmin, CashAggregateAdmin, EvidencePackageAdmin

## HTMX Endpoints

- GET `/finance/transparency/disclosure/{id}/review` - Review disclosure with aggregate details
- POST `/finance/transparency/referral/{id}/send` - Submit evidence package to CBK/FRC

## Models (Managed=False)

- CandidateDisclosure - Candidate financial records
- CashAggregate - Suspicious cash flow aggregates (read-only from analysis)
- EvidencePackage - Investigation evidence collections

## Environment Variables

- `DJANGO_SECRET_KEY` - Django secret key
- `DATABASE_URL` - PostgreSQL connection string
- `CBK_FRC_ADAPTER_URL` - CBK Financial Reporting Centre submission endpoint
- `TRANSPARENCY_API_URL` - Transparency service API endpoint

## Permissions

FINANCE_TRANSPARENCY_OFFICER, FRC_LIAISON roles via SpiceDB. Officers compile evidence; FRC liaison submits to authorities.

## Transparency & Timing

- Disclosures are published with a delay (post-election) to avoid influencing voters
- Evidence packages are confidential until FRC determination
- CBK/FRC referrals are formal legal submissions and require supervisor approval

## Notes

- Cash aggregates are automatically generated by anomaly detection service
- All referrals are logged for audit and EACC coordination
- Evidence must be original documents or cryptographically signed copies
