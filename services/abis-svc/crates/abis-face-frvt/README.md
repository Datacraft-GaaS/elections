# eel-abis-face-frvt

NIST FRVT evaluation harness for the EEL ABIS pipeline.

Implements 1:1 (FMR/FNMR), 1:N (Rank-N hit rate), quality correlation, and
per-demographic fairness slices (age band, sex, Fitzpatrick skin tone).

## References

- NIST FRVT Ongoing. frvt.nist.gov.
- Phillips et al. (2011). An Introduction to the Good, the Bad, & the Ugly
  Face Recognition Challenge Problem. FG 2011.
- Fitzpatrick (1988). The validity and practicality of sun-reactive skin types
  I through VI. Arch Dermatol 124(6).
