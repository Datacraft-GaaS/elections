# eel-abis-face-occlusion

Occlusion-robust face matching for the EEL ABIS pipeline.

Skin-tone segmentation detects mask/glasses/hijab/hand occlusions. An
attention-gate mechanism suppresses corrupted embedding dimensions.

## References

- Song et al. (2019). Occlusion Robust Face Recognition Based on Mask Learning. ICCV.
- Zhao et al. (2018). Towards Pose Invariant Face Recognition in the Wild. CVPR.
- Klum et al. (2014). Soft biometric attributes from facial shape. IEEE BTAS.
