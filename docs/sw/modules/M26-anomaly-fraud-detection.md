# M26 — Anomaly and Fraud Detection Stack

**Module ID:** M26
**Name:** Seven-Layer Anomaly, Fraud, and Rigging Detection Stack
**Runs on:** Kit-side (Layers 1-2 edge models) + Central ML cluster (Layers 2-6) + Human Adjudication Console (Layer 7)
**Owner:** Datacraft
**Status:** Draft v2
**IPR:** Custom Software vests in IEBC per GCC 15.4; OSS components (scikit-learn, PyOD) under BSD/Apache licences

## 1. Purpose

Implement a defence-in-depth seven-layer detection stack where a rigging attempt must defeat every independent layer to succeed. Layers range from real-time kit telemetry anomaly detection through distributional forensics, cryptographic reproducibility checks, RLA linkage, cross-source triangulation, and human-adjudicated tip lines. No layer grants autonomous authority; every alert routes to a human decision point.

Strategy reference: §XIX.1 + §XIII + §XIV.

## 2. User Personas

- **Election Command Centre (ECC) Analyst** — monitors real-time Layers 1-2-6-7 alert queue; dispatches field responses
- **IAB Statistical Auditor** — operates Layers 3-5; reviews distributional analysis and RLA linkage
- **Datacraft SOC Analyst** — monitors Layer 4 (cryptographic/reproducibility) and system health
- **KEOG/ELOG Observer** — provides Layer 6 PVT data; views Layer 6 triangulation results read-only
- **ECC Commander (IEBC senior officer)** — receives escalated alerts; authorises result holds
- **DCI/ODPP** — receives election-offence evidence packages generated by Layer 7
- **Whistleblower / Tip Submitter** — provides Layer 7 inputs via M23 grievance channels
- **Field Officer** — dispatched by ECC in response to Layer 1/2 alerts

## 3. Functional Requirements

### Layer 1 — Kit-Side Self-Monitoring (Real-Time, Per-Kit)

- **FR-M26-001** Kit telemetry stream: every 60 seconds, each kit emits a signed telemetry packet containing: authentication count, authentication success rate, throughput (voters/hour), failure-reason distribution, battery level, GPS coordinates, network connectivity state, storage free, temperature, reboot count, manual-override count.
- **FR-M26-002** Kit-side anomaly detection: on-device Isolation Forest model (INT8 quantised, <= 5 MB) computes anomaly score on the last 60-minute rolling window. Model trained on prior-election baselines. Alert thresholds: throughput outlier (> 3 sigma above kit's 30-minute baseline); authentication-time distribution shift (KL-divergence > 0.5 from hourly baseline); geolocation drift outside geofence; unusual sequence of manual overrides (> 3 in 10 minutes); unexpected reboot.
- **FR-M26-003** Kit-side alert: on crossing threshold, kit generates a Layer 1 alert event (signed, appended to Merkle chain), transmits to ECC, and surfaces a subtle in-kit indicator to the presiding officer without alarming voters.
- **FR-M26-004** ECC alert dashboard: Layer 1 alerts appear in the ECC queue within 2 minutes; colour-coded by severity; one-click dispatch of field officer; alert includes GPS coordinates, current telemetry, and anomaly score.

### Layer 2 — Cross-Station Statistical Monitoring (Real-Time, Per-Constituency)

- **FR-M26-005** Central unsupervised ensemble: Isolation Forest + One-Class SVM + Autoencoder trained on prior-election station-level patterns. Five dimensions: turnout, authentication-time distribution, rejected-ballot rate, biometric-first-try-success rate, manual-override rate.
- **FR-M26-006** Peer comparison: every station scored against the distribution of all stations in its constituency every 10 minutes. Alert trigger: any station in the top 0.5% of peer-deviation on any single dimension or top 1% on any two dimensions simultaneously.
- **FR-M26-007** Layer 2 alert: routed to ECC within 5 minutes of trigger; includes peer-comparison plot, dimensional scores, contributing features. Advisory flag on the result-publication pipeline (does not hold result automatically; ECC Commander decision required for hold).
- **FR-M26-008** False-positive tuning: model recalibrated at T-30 dry-run on synthetic data; ECC analyst feedback mechanism during election day to reduce alert fatigue.

### Layer 3 — Statistical Distributional Analysis (Post-Close, Per-Station)

- **FR-M26-009** Last-digit uniformity test (Beber and Scacco 2012, AJPS 56(1)): applied to final digits of per-station candidate tallies where count >= 25. Expected uniform distribution over digits 0-9. Chi-squared test at p < 0.05 flags the station. Methodology published alongside results.
- **FR-M26-010** Turnout-vote-share scatter analysis (Rozenas 2017; Klimek et al 2012, PNAS): bivariate histogram applied to constituency-level turnout vs winner-vote-share scatter; linear correlation coefficient and cluster analysis published. Advisory only.
- **FR-M26-011** Benford 2BL caveat: Benford's Law applied only to derived quantities (margins, ratios) where mathematical preconditions hold (see Deckert, Myagkov, Ordeshook 2011, Electoral Studies); never to raw vote counts per station. Methodology published.
- **FR-M26-012** Layer 3 report: generated within 4 hours of all Form 34As received; published on the public portal and delivered to IAB and KEOG/ELOG simultaneously.
- **FR-M26-013** No Layer 3 result triggers autonomous action. IAB reviews all flagged stations; can request a targeted recount per Elections Act s.80.

### Layer 4 — Cryptographic and Reproducibility Checks (Post-Close, Whole-Tally)

- **FR-M26-014** Merkle-inclusion check: for every Form 34A submitted, verify its hash is included in the constituency Form 34B Merkle root and that root in the Form 34C root. Any missing inclusion proof = Layer 4 critical alert to Datacraft SOC + IEBC Chair.
- **FR-M26-015** Hash-chain continuity: verify that the sequence of Merkle nodes from station open to Form 34A publish is unbroken; any gap = Layer 4 critical alert.
- **FR-M26-016** Reproducibility check: re-run the full national aggregation from raw Form 34A JSON inputs and compare to published Form 34C bit-identically. Any variance = Layer 4 critical alert published immediately.
- **FR-M26-017** Cryptographic canary: Datacraft publishes known-good canary transactions in the RTS pipeline daily from T-30. Canaries are Merkle-anchored with a published timestamp. Any canary-hash mismatch detected = Layer 4 critical alert + public announcement within 15 minutes.
- **FR-M26-018** All Layer 4 alerts are public: published to the public portal and IAB within 15 minutes of detection. No suppression mechanism exists.

### Layer 5 — RLA Linkage

- **FR-M26-019** Layer 5 is the RLA module (M21). Its output (CONFIRMED / RECOUNT_REQUIRED) is the Layer 5 signal in the detection stack.
- **FR-M26-020** A Layer 5 RECOUNT_REQUIRED event automatically creates a Layer 5 critical alert in the detection stack, cross-referenced with any Layer 2 or Layer 3 flags at the affected stations.
- **FR-M26-021** Layer 5 alerts are published to the public portal immediately with the statistical justification.

### Layer 6 — Cross-Source Triangulation (Real-Time, Whole-Country)

- **FR-M26-022** Ingest parallel vote tabulation (PVT) results from KEOG/ELOG via a signed API endpoint. Map PVT station codes to KIEMS station codes. Compare candidate tallies per station.
- **FR-M26-023** Alert triggers: any divergence > 2% at any constituency; any divergence > 0.5% at national level; any station where KIEMS result differs from PVT result by > 10 votes per candidate.
- **FR-M26-024** Presiding-officer manual count cross-check: cross-reference kit-entered tallies with the separately uploaded presiding-officer manual count (captured at close on a separate tablet). Any discrepancy > 0 triggers a Layer 6 flag.
- **FR-M26-025** Party-agent independent tally cross-check: if a party agent submits an independently tallied Form 34A via the party-agent portal, cross-reference against KIEMS tally. Divergence > 0 votes per candidate triggers Layer 6 flag.
- **FR-M26-026** Layer 6 alerts include: divergence value, diverging sources, affected station/constituency, and a recommendation (hold result pending review / proceed with explanation). ECC Commander decides.

### Layer 7 — Human-Adjudicated Tip Line and Observer Signals (Real-Time, All-Cycle)

- **FR-M26-027** Ingests: grievances from M23 (Public Verifier, short-code, WhatsApp, SMS, kit kiosk, in-person); observer field reports from KEOG, ELOG, IFES, Carter Center, EU EOM via signed reporting API; media investigative signals (manual entry by ECC analyst); whistleblower reports (M23 protected channel).
- **FR-M26-028** Signal aggregation: when three or more independent sources report the same type of issue at the same station or constituency, system generates a Layer 7 convergence alert. Convergence threshold configurable by IAB pre-election.
- **FR-M26-029** Layer 7 adjudication: ECC analyst reviews convergence alert with all contributing signals; marks each signal VERIFIED / UNVERIFIED / FALSE_POSITIVE; if >= 2 signals verified, escalates to ECC Commander.
- **FR-M26-030** ECC Commander decision options: hold result pending field investigation; dispatch field officer; refer to DCI/ODPP; clear with reason code. All decisions logged and published within 24 hours of alert close.
- **FR-M26-031** Election Offences Act 2016 evidence-package generation: verified Layer 7 convergence alert with >= 2 independent verified sources auto-generates an evidence package (via M23) for DCI referral within 6 hours.

### Cross-Layer

- **FR-M26-032** Unified alert console: ECC sees all seven layers in a single dashboard; alerts sorted by layer, severity, and time; cross-layer correlation surfaced automatically (e.g., a station appearing in Layer 1, Layer 2, and Layer 7 simultaneously is auto-elevated to CRITICAL).
- **FR-M26-033** No layer grants autonomous ballot rejection, result annulment, or voter exclusion. Every alert is advisory until a named human decision is made and logged.
- **FR-M26-034** All alert events and resolutions appended to the Merkle audit chain.
- **FR-M26-035** Public-facing detection report: published within T+4 hours of Form 34C publication; includes Layer 3 distributional analysis, Layer 4 reproducibility result, Layer 6 divergence summary, and Layer 7 convergence summary. Layer 1 and Layer 2 raw telemetry published in aggregate.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements

- **NFR-M26-007** Availability of central detection cluster: >= 99.95% during T-7 to T+4.

## 5. User Stories

1. As an ECC Analyst, I see a Layer 1 alert for a station in Nairobi that processed 47 voters in 10 minutes, with a GPS showing it is 400m outside its geofence, and I dispatch a field officer within 5 minutes.
2. As an IAB Statistical Auditor, I receive the Layer 3 report 4 hours after the last Form 34A and identify three stations where last-digit analysis shows p < 0.001; I request targeted ballot-level scrutiny for those stations.
3. As a member of the public, I download the Layer 4 reproduce-me bundle and re-run the national aggregation on my laptop in 90 minutes, confirming no reproducibility variance.
4. As an ELOG observer, I submit a PVT batch via the signed API; the system cross-references it against KIEMS tallies within 5 minutes and shows no divergences > 2%.
5. As an ECC Commander, I see a CRITICAL cross-layer alert for a constituency appearing in Layers 2, 6, and 7 simultaneously; I issue a result hold pending field investigation.
6. As a Datacraft SOC Analyst, I see a Layer 4 cryptographic canary mismatch at 15:23; I trigger the public alert within 10 minutes and escalate to IEBC Chair.
7. As an IAB member, I confirm that no AI model made any autonomous decision to reject a ballot, exclude a voter, or modify a tally throughout the election.
8. As an ODPP Prosecutor, I receive a Layer 7 evidence package citing three verified independent sources of bribery at the same station, all cross-referenced by timestamp and GPS.
9. As an ECC Analyst, I mark a Layer 2 alert as a false positive (the station has a high throughput because it is a large-population urban centre); the model receives feedback to adjust its peer-comparison baseline.
10. As the IEBC Chair, I review the public detection report at T+4 hours and note zero Layer 4 alerts and three Layer 7 alerts all cleared by field investigation.

class DetectionAlert(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra='forbid', validate_by_name=True)
    id: str
    layer: Literal["L1","L2","L3","L4","L5","L6","L7"]
    severity: Literal["INFO","WARNING","CRITICAL"]
    station_code: str | None
    constituency_code: str | None
    description: str
    contributing_signals: list[str]     # IDs of source events / tip IDs
    anomaly_score: float | None
    dimensional_scores: dict[str, float] | None   # e.g. {"turnout": 3.1, "override_rate": 2.8}
    generated_at: datetime
    assigned_analyst_id: str | None
    resolution: Literal["OPEN","INVESTIGATING","CLEARED","ESCALATED","HELD","REFERRED"] | None
    resolution_note: str | None
    resolved_at: datetime | None
    merkle_node_id: str | None
    is_public: bool

class KitTelemetryPacket(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra='forbid', validate_by_name=True)
    id: str
    kit_serial: str
    station_code: str
    auth_count: int
    auth_success_rate: float
    throughput_per_hour: float
    failure_reason_distribution: dict[str, int]
    battery_pct: int
    latitude: float
    longitude: float
    network_state: Literal["CONNECTED","DEGRADED","OFFLINE"]
    storage_free_mb: int
    temperature_c: float
    reboot_count: int
    manual_override_count: int
    on_device_anomaly_score: float
    packet_at: datetime
    kit_signature: str              # HSM-signed hex

class CrossSourceComparison(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra='forbid', validate_by_name=True)
    id: str
    station_code: str
    kiems_candidate_tallies: dict[str, int]
    pvt_candidate_tallies: dict[str, int] | None
    manual_count_tallies: dict[str, int] | None
    party_agent_tallies: dict[str, int] | None
    max_divergence_pct: float
    layer6_alert_id: str | None
    compared_at: datetime

class DistributionalAnalysisResult(BaseModel):
    model_config = ConfigDict(extra='forbid', validate_by_name=True)
    id: str
    contest_id: str
    method: Literal["BEBER_SCACCO_2BL","ROZENAS_SCATTER","KLIMEK_BIVARIATE"]
    stations_analysed: int
    stations_flagged: int
    p_value_distribution: dict[str, float]  # p-value per flagged station
    methodology_note: str
    published_at: datetime | None
    merkle_node_id: str | None

## 12. Test Strategy

- **Unit:** Isolation Forest / One-Class SVM / Autoencoder inference pipeline; last-digit chi-squared test; Merkle inclusion verification; convergence threshold logic.
- **Integration:** Layer 1 telemetry pipeline from kit to ECC; Layer 6 PVT ingestion; Layer 7 tip aggregation.
- **E2E:** simulated election with injected anomalies at layers 1-7; verify all seven layers fire correctly; verify no false autonomous action.
- **Statistical validation:** compare Layer 3 outputs against known synthetic fraud patterns; false-positive rate calibration on prior-election data.
- **Security:** attempt to suppress a Layer 4 alert via API; attempt to inject a false Layer 6 PVT batch; attempt to modify a detection alert post-resolution.
- **Accessibility:** ECC console WCAG 2.2 AA (analysts may work with disabilities).
- **Load:** 55,393 simultaneous telemetry packets on election-day peak; Layer 2 computation under full load.
- **Red team:** T-90 adversarial test — red team attempts to bypass all seven layers simultaneously.

## 15. Risks + Mitigations

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| ML model produces excessive false positives causing ECC alert fatigue | High | Medium | Conservative detection thresholds; T-90 dry-run calibration; ECC analyst feedback loop; analyst can snooze repeated false-positive patterns for a station with reason code |
| Adversary studies the detection algorithm and designs an attack that evades all seven layers | Low | Critical | Layers use independent signals and different accountability chains; an attacker must coordinate the kit, the distribution analysis, the cryptographic chain, the RLA sample, cross-source triangulation, and the human tip line simultaneously; this is operationally infeasible |
| Layer 3 distributional analysis used as petition evidence without methodology disclosure | Medium | Medium | Methodology published alongside every Layer 3 report; caveat on Benford's Law misapplication prominently included; IAB statistician named as expert witness if required |
| KEOG/ELOG PVT data not available in time for Layer 6 | Medium | Medium | Layer 6 degrades gracefully without PVT input; manual count cross-check and party-agent tally remain active; divergence alert suppressed with a "data unavailable" flag rather than a false clear |
| Layer 4 canary mismatch causes public panic before root cause is established | Low | High | Canary mismatch alert includes a preliminary explanation field; Datacraft SOC has a 15-minute post-alert window to add a root-cause note before public publication; "mismatch under investigation" is the initial public message |
